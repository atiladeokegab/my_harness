# Take the reason behind a choice, then adapt it

Source: Austin Kleon, *Steal Like an Artist* (Workman, 2012), ch. 2 "Don't Wait Until You Know Who You
Are to Get Started". Paraphrased in our words; anything quoted is marked and at most one sentence.

**Principle.** From a reference, take why it made a choice and rebuild that reason in our product's
terms; copying its surface (its values, its layout) produces a knockoff.

## Why it holds

- Ch. 2: copying is practice by reverse engineering, like a mechanic taking a car apart to see how it
  runs. The aim is to see the way the hero sees, not to look like the hero.
- Ch. 2: mimicking the surface without knowing where it came from never rises above a knockoff.
- Ch. 2: imitation copies; emulation goes one step further into your own thing. Kleon's example is an
  athlete whose borrowed moves only worked once he reshaped them for his own body.
- Ch. 2: where your copy falls short of the hero is where your own work lives; amplify that gap
  rather than closing it.
- For us: our body is different from the reference's (our subject, our content, our two sizes, our
  users). A trait that ignores that difference fails R2 and usually B6.

## What to check

- Take the reference apart before borrowing: its type scale, spacing steps, accent use, grid and the
  job its first screen does, written as decisions, not adjectives.
- Each borrowed trait is written in three parts: the reference does X; it does so because Y; for our
  product Y becomes Z.
- Z differs from X in a way you can see: a different value, medium or place on the screen.

## Pass or fail test

Apply to the chosen direction's `## Direction contract` and the trait list it came from.

- **PASS:** every borrowed trait carries the reference's reason and our adapted form, and no adapted
  form reuses the reference's exact value (hex, font, radius, layout) unless the contract says why our
  subject needs the same one.
- **FAIL:** a trait's only reason is that a reference does it, or an exact value is lifted with no
  reason of our own.

Observable: the X / because Y / becomes Z line for each trait, and a diff of values against the
reference captures.
