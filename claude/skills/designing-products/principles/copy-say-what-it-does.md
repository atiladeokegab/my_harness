# Say what it does to what

Sources: GOV.UK, "A to Z style guide" (words to avoid; metaphors) and "Writing guidelines: Use clear
language", guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk; Digital.gov, "Plain language guide series: Clear and short"
(adapted from PlainLanguage.gov), digital.gov/guides/plain-language. Paraphrased in our words; anything
quoted is marked and at most one sentence.

**Principle.** Name the product's own things, the nouns a user can point at, and give each a verb that
says what happens to it. No abstraction stands in for the product.

## Why it holds

- GOV.UK makes plain English mandatory and lists words to avoid (empower, facilitate, leverage,
  streamline, transform, deliver, among 31), each with a concrete replacement: they name an intention,
  not an act.
- GOV.UK on metaphors: "they do not say what you actually mean and lead to slower comprehension of
  your content." "Unlock", "elevate" and "one-stop shop" are metaphors.
- Active voice says who does what (Digital.gov) and keeps sentences short (GOV.UK). "Your report is
  generated" hides the actor; "the service signs the report" does not.
- A visitor decides from what the product does to their thing. A sentence built on "solution",
  "platform", "experience" or "workflow" fits any product, so it tells them nothing (rubric T7).

## What to check on a screen

- Each headline and lead sentence holds a noun from the product's vocabulary (`DESIGN.md` `## Voice`)
  and a verb you could film: compares, signs, flags, sends, deletes.
- The swap test: put a competitor's name in the sentence. If it is still true and still says nothing
  specific, it fails.
- Active voice wherever the actor matters, and the actor is named.

## Pass or fail test

Apply to each capture on its own: desktop 1280x633 and phone 390x757. Read the main heading, the first
three sentences and every button.

- **PASS:** each one names a product thing and a concrete act, and fails the swap test.
- **FAIL:** quote each one whose only nouns are abstractions or whose verb is on GOV.UK's words to avoid,
  and the swap test result.

Observable: the quoted lines, or "none".
