# Choose what to leave out, and write it down

Source: Austin Kleon, *Steal Like an Artist* (Workman, 2012), ch. 10 "Creativity Is Subtraction".
Paraphrased in our words; anything quoted is marked and at most one sentence.

**Principle.** A direction names what it refuses as plainly as what it uses, and works inside a declared
limit; the left-out list binds every screen, like the palette does.

## Why it holds

- Ch. 10: when everything is available, the work that stands out comes from deciding what to omit.
  Unlimited options do not free a maker; they freeze them.
- Ch. 10: a self-imposed limit is the way out of a block: one colour, a short window, only the
  materials at hand. Kleon's example is a children's author who, on a bet, wrote a bestseller using
  only fifty distinct words.
- Ch. 10: people respond to a maker working against a limit, and what is not shown is often what makes
  the work interesting. Creative work is the sum of what is put in and what is kept out.
- For us: the median AI screen is the sum of everything the category usually has (hero, three feature
  cards, stats, logos, testimonials). Nobody chose to leave those out, so they all arrive. An event's
  hours and our one subject are limits to design with, not excuses.

## What to check

- The direction contract has a **Leaves out** list: concrete elements that the quiet
  category-standard card carries and this direction does not.
- It names one hard limit with its reason, such as one accent, one face, one image, or no cards.
- Every specimen and every built screen shows none of the excluded elements.
- At critique, name what to remove from a failing screen before proposing anything to add.

## Pass or fail test

Apply to the chosen direction's `## Direction contract` and its specimen captures at 1280x633 and 390x757.

- **PASS:** the Leaves out list names three or more concrete elements present on the category-standard
  card (our threshold), the contract states one hard limit with a reason, and no capture shows an
  excluded element.
- **FAIL:** no list; an item too vague to check ("clutter", "noise"); no hard limit; or a capture shows
  something the list excludes.

Observable: the Leaves out list beside the standard card's elements, and each capture checked against it.

## Used at

**Look** (step 3: the cards and the direction contract). **Critique:** the first fix is a removal.
