# Take from many sources, never from one

Source: Austin Kleon, *Steal Like an Artist* (Workman, 2012), ch. 1 "Steal Like an Artist" and ch. 2
"Don't Wait Until You Know Who You Are to Get Started". Paraphrased in our words; anything quoted is
marked and at most one sentence.

**Principle.** A direction is a blend of several references, some from outside the product's category,
each chosen for one named thing worth taking; a direction built on one reference is a knockoff.

## Why it holds

- Ch. 1: nothing is made from nothing; new work is a remix of earlier work, and what people call
  original is usually work whose sources they have not seen. Two ideas side by side make a third.
- Ch. 1: a collector is not a hoarder. Keep only what is worth taking, from a wide range (film,
  architecture, signage, the physical world), because the work is only as good as what went in.
- Ch. 2: borrowing from one hero gets you called the next version of them; borrowing from many reads as
  your own voice. Kleon's good theft takes from many and credits them; bad theft takes from one.
- For us: the blind judge scores R2 at 0 when a specimen reads as one of the references
  (`reference/rubric.md` §2). This file is how a direction avoids that before it is drawn.

## What to check

- The reference set has a line per item naming the one thing worth taking from it; an item with no
  such line is dropped, not kept "for inspiration".
- At least one source sits outside the product's category: one of the seven things from the subject's
  world, or a craft that solves the same problem in another medium.
- Each rough line lists its sources with what each gives.

## Pass or fail test

Apply to each rough line in the planning file's §UI (the tournament's round 0), before any sketch is drawn.

- **PASS:** the line names three or more sources, at least one from outside the product's category,
  and no single source supplies more than half of its listed traits (our thresholds).
- **FAIL:** fewer than three sources, all sources are products in the same category, one source supplies
  most traits, or a trait has no source and no reason.

Observable: each card's trait list with a source beside every trait, and the count per source.

## Used at

**Look** (step 3, the direction tournament: the seven things and the 30 rough lines). **Intent:** the reference
list is the first collection.
