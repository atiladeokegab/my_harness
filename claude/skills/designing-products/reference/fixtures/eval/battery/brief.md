# Eval brief 2: Hartwell Battery Desk (a data dashboard, Operate)

Synthetic: a community energy co-op that does not exist. Every number is illustrative.

- **Product.** Hartwell Battery Desk: the one screen the duty operator of Hartwell Community Energy uses to
  decide when the co-op's 2 MWh battery at Hartwell Mill sends its charge to the grid tonight.
- **Audience.** Six volunteer duty operators (two are retired electrical engineers), at a laptop in the
  mill office or on a phone at home, once a day around 15:00.
- **Job.** Pick tonight's discharge window: the half-hours where the price is highest and the village
  needs it, without draining the battery below the 20% the warranty needs.
- **Action.** "Discharge 1.2 MWh from 17:30 to 19:30": one button that schedules it.
- **Proof.** The state of charge now, tonight's half-hourly prices and the village's forecast demand, and
  what the window earns.
- **The one feeling.** A working control room, quiet and exact; not a trading app.
- **The one memorable thing.** The evening's 48 half-hour settlement periods as one strip, the chosen
  window cut out of it.
- **Mode.** Operate, on every screen.
- **Field.** `data-dashboards`. It has no Operate entry in the library, so the refs are Persuade first
  screens that show a real dashboard (`../../../library.md` rule 2): `data-dashboards/plausible` (one
  honest dashboard as the hero), `data-dashboards/retool` (dashboards from components),
  `data-dashboards/datawrapper` (restrained charts), `data-dashboards/our-world-in-data` (Read: chart-first,
  sourced numbers). Taste set: Linear, Stripe.
- **Seven things from the subject's world** (round 0): the substation's single-line diagram; the
  battery container's state-of-charge gauge; the 48 half-hour settlement periods; the evening demand
  hump on a load curve; the duty log book on the mill desk; the network operator's export-limit notice;
  the mill's leat and its old water wheel.
- **Standard card.** `standard.html`: browser defaults, a heading, the facts in a table, the button.
