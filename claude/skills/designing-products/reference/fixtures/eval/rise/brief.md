# Eval brief 3: Rise (a consumer app)

Synthetic: an app that does not exist. Times and weights are illustrative.

- **Product.** Rise: a phone app that tells a home baker when their sourdough starter will peak, and when
  to feed it next.
- **Audience.** Home bakers with one jar on the kitchen counter, phone in a floury hand, mornings and
  evenings. Phone first; the desktop page is the same app on a laptop.
- **Job.** Know when the starter is at its peak, ready to bake with, and when to feed it.
- **Action.** "Log this feed": one button that records the feed and sets the next reminder.
- **Proof.** How high the starter has risen since its last feed, when it will peak, and the last week of
  feeds.
- **The one feeling.** A kitchen notebook kept by someone patient; warm, not cute.
- **The one memorable thing.** The jar, with the rubber band marking where the starter began and the
  level it has reached.
- **Mode.** Persuade on the first screen, Operate below it.
- **Field.** `consumer-apps`. Library refs (`../../../library.md`): `consumer-apps/things` (craft and
  restraint), `consumer-apps/halide` (precise, measured), `consumer-apps/bear` (warm editorial),
  `consumer-apps/carrot-weather` (an owned voice). Taste set: Linear, Stripe.
- **Seven things from the subject's world** (round 0): the jar and the rubber band at the starting
  level; masking tape with the date in marker; the flour bags (strong white, rye); the kitchen scale
  reading in grams; the bubbles and the domed top at peak; the baker's notebook of times; the oven dial.
- **Standard card.** `standard.html`: browser defaults, a heading, the facts in a table, the button.
