# Fixture brief: Strata Lifts (synthetic)

The surface brief the direction judge gets with the trio. Synthetic: a feature of our own product, Strata;
the lift times are illustrative, not a timetable.

- **Product.** Strata Lifts: one page that says when Tower Bridge next opens for a ship, and what is passing.
- **Audience.** Londoners crossing the bridge on foot or by bus, and visitors on the riverside who want to
  see a lift. Phone first, outdoors, often in daylight.
- **Job.** Know, at a glance, the next lift time and whether it is worth waiting for.
- **Action.** "Notify me before it lifts": one button.
- **Proof.** The next lift, the vessel's name and direction, and today's other lifts.
- **The one feeling.** The bridge is a working machine, not a postcard.
- **The one memorable thing.** The bascules, drawn at the angle they will open to.
- **Mode.** Persuade on the first screen, Operate below it.
- **References (stand-ins, our own screens).** `refs/strata-desktop.png` and `refs/strata-mobile.png` (Strata's
  home), `refs/refinery-desktop.png` and `refs/refinery-mobile.png` (Refinery's home). They stand in for the
  best real products in the subject's world, which a real run captures into `$HUB_DIR/projects/<p>/refs/`.
- **Specimens to rank.** Three directions and the category standard, named by the parent (`specimen-1` to
  `specimen-3` in a shuffled order, and `standard`), each at `-desktop.png` (1280x633) and `-mobile.png` (390x757).
